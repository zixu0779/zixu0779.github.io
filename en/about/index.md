### Development

I’ve made a few desktop tools, and lately I’ve been trying my hand at game development.

**[OneDrop](https://github.com/zixu0779/OneDrop)** is an Edge extension I use to send text and files between my devices through OneDrive.

**[DungeonTavern · 地下酒馆](https://github.com/zixu0779/DungeonTavern)** is a Unity game demo I’m working on. It’s set in a tavern that does business in a dungeon.

An earlier project of mine, [Sentences-Desktop](https://github.com/noneSycamore/Sentences-Desktop), is a little Windows desktop window that displays a quote.

### Fiction

I read a bit of everything. Mostly fanfic, across all sorts of fandoms. I’ve also got a soft spot for Western-style fantasy.

Sometimes I’ll pick up a story about someone’s working life, or a mystery. Every now and then I’ll go for historical fiction or wuxia for a change of pace.
