The short version: I’m at the University of Sydney doing the Master of Computer Science (Advanced). It’s the end of semester two, 2024, and exams are nearly here.

# Studying

Time is tight. There’s a lot of practical work here, though I’ve written my share of reports too.

Classes usually come in two flavours. In a lecture, one professor teaches a whole room of students. A tutorial is smaller and more hands-on: one tutor to a dozen or so students.

The assignments come one after another, and they’re often a headache. Some courses have weekly tasks on top of that. It’s hard to get a breather during the week, and I usually have to set aside a day of the weekend for coursework.

I’ve learned a lot, though. Some reports taught me precious little about the subject, but at least my written English got better. And reports are only a small part of it. Quite a few of the assignments have been genuinely interesting and helped me figure things out.

# Everyday life

By my reckoning, most things here cost about five times what they do back home. A few things cost about the same.

On an ordinary day I spend upwards of 150 yuan on food alone. I can live with that.

The rent is what gets me. I pay 2,100 yuan a week, or 8,400 for four weeks, and that’s about as low as I’ve seen in Sydney. Paying less would mean competing for one of the few university rooms. Even those run to around 1,600 a week. Still a lot.

# Games

After finishing the main story of The Witcher 3 and Hearts of Stone, I completely lost the urge to play anything. I even bought Black Myth: Wukong and haven’t started it yet...

The Witcher 3 really is that good. It’s about a decade old and the textures show their age, but the story, the mechanics, the combat — I had fun with all of it. Especially the story. One choice can open up so many possibilities. It was the first time I’d really felt how a story could branch and weave back together. Even the little side quests and contracts were worth doing.

Oh, and I can’t exactly leave out Gwent 🐶.

I can see why people felt Cyberpunk 2077 fell short of what CD Projekt Red could do. The mountain of bugs at launch didn’t help, but even now that the game runs much better, its story just doesn’t come close to The Witcher 3 for me.
(Phantom Liberty was pretty good, though.)

# Plans for the summer

My main plan is to work on a university project. It should be fairly relaxed, at least compared with this semester.
